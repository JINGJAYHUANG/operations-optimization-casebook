# Roadmap

## 0.2

- Solver-adapter contract for OR-Tools, HiGHS, CBC, and commercial solvers.
- Dual values and reduced-cost evidence where supported.
- Multi-period production and inventory planning.
- Time-window routing and skill-based workforce scheduling.
- JUnit and SARIF-compatible audit exports.

## 0.3

- Stochastic-programming scenario trees.
- Distributionally robust examples.
- Simulation-optimization validation loop.
- Post-implementation realized-versus-modeled tracking.

## Not promised

The project will not present a synthetic optimum as a real operating instruction or allow solver output to bypass human accountability.
